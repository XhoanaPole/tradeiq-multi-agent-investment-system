How TradeIQ Works
1

Research Analyst Agent

Scans real-time financial news, press releases, and SEC filings. Uses Vector RAG to retrieve relevant contextual company documents and evaluate market sentiment.

2

Quant Analyst Agent

Extracts price history charts and crunches key financial metrics (P/E ratio, Debt/Equity, margins) to verify underlying quantitative health and trend trajectory.

3

Risk Manager Agent

Runs a comprehensive risk checklist mapping out potential red flags, debt issues, or compliance risks, reporting critical exposure points.

4

Report Writer Agent

Synthesizes research, quant outputs, and risk assessments into a clean, human-readable Markdown investment brief.

5

Evaluator Agent

Scores the investment signal strength 0–10 based on fundamentals, sentiment, and risk level. The score is shown to you so you can decide to approve, reject, or request a revision.
